How the games time a fight

Every game in the Frame Data tab, side by side: how long attacks take to come out, how long they stay out, how long they leave you open, and how early each game lets you cancel or evade. Pick which games to compare in the bar above.

Units. All timings are in frames at 60 fps (1 f ≈ 16.7 ms). Elden Ring times its animations at 30 fps, so its frames are doubled here; hover any mark for the game's own frames. Middle values are medians, so a few extreme moves don't drag them.
